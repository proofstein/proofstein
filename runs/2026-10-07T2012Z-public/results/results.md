## Precision
Detection rate on its own is gameable: a generator that claimed every algorithm at
every line of every file would be credited with finding everything.

**Claim precision is the check on that**, not component precision. Component
precision can be defeated by packing the guesses into a few components -- ten
components each carrying an occurrence for every line reach full recall at 80%
component precision. Counting individual evidence claims prices every guess: the
same document credits 19 claims out of 7670.

An honest generator makes about one claim per site it found, so its two figures sit
close together. A wide gap between them is the signature of guessing.
